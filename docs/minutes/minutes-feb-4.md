### Discussion and notes

#### Brainstorming, requirements
**General:**

- The system shall allow users to log in. (F)
- The system shall only allow the user to access the main page upon logging in. (NF)
- The system shall load all products on the main page within 2 seconds. (NF)
- The system shall keep track of stock quantities of items. (F)
- The system shall enable a user to complete the flow of logging in to checking out in less than 10 minutes. (NF)
- The system shall allow users to view the products. (F)

**Staff:**

- The clothing store system shall allow the manager to update the clothing inventory. (F)
- The system shall allow the employee to assign themselves to a certain order to fulfill (F).
- The system shall allow the manager to assign pending orders to employees. (F)
- The system shall allow the manager to view all orders that have been created (current, past, and cancelled). (F)

**Orders:**

- The system shall allow the modification or cancellation of an order before delivery. (F)
- The system shall not allow orders to be cancelled or modified within 24 hours prior to the delivery date. (NF) ?
- The system shall only allow an order to have a delivery date that is 24 hours after it has been placed. (NF) (excluded)
- The system shall allow only one manager account to exist. (NF)

**Customer:**

- The system shall allow customers to add and edit items to their cart. (F)
- The system shall allow users to view their order history (current, past, and cancelled) and their status. (F)
- The system shall reward users with loyalty points upon a successful order. (F)
- The system shall allow customers to view the information of a desired item. (F)
- The system shall allow customers to checkout orders. (F)
