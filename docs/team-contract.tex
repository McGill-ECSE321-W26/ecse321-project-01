\section*{Roles and Responsibilities}
\subsection*{What are the roles needed for this team to be successful?}
We are going to need the following roles:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Documentation manager
    \item Project manager (scrum master)
    \item Software lead
    \item Creative director
\end{itemize}
\subsection*{How are we assigning these roles?}
We are assigning these roles on a voluntary basis, also taking into consideration the strengths and weaknesses of every member of the team. For example, since Santiago enjoys writing and maintaining documentation, he will be the documentation manager. Jason, on the other hand, has more of an artistic style, so he will be the creative director. Similarly, we will assign the other roles to the other members of the team.  
\subsection*{How will we keep each other accountable?}
Since this is a team project, it is crucial that everyone gets their assigned job done, as we need the contributions of everyone to successfully proceed in the project. In order to keep each other accountable, we will as a team keep checks on the contributions of all members, ensuring that everyone is progressing well with their work, and helping each other if we need to.
\subsection*{How will we keep ourselves organized as a team?}
First, the documentation manager will ensure that the project documentation is up to date so that everyone can always refer to it when they need a refresher on progress. We will also be using the functionalities of GitHub to keep track of work to do, and the development that is made. Finally, we will communicate as a group to ensure that everyone is on the same page. 

\section*{Communication}
\subsection*{What is our preferred means of communication?}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item We will communicate via:
    a Discord group chat, where we can discuss via text or video call
    \item Our expectations for response-time between communications is:
    for general discussions, 48 hours. As we get close to the deadline, 24 hours or less.
\end{enumerate}
\subsection*{What are the measures our team will take to minimize miscommunication?}
To minimize miscommunication, we will make sure to send all important in the group chat, even if we already mentioned them in person. That way, everyone can have access to the message and the information. We will also have a team member (probably the documentation manager) take notes during meetings, and these notes will be available to everyone as part of the project Wiki. This way, no information will be lost.
\subsection*{If we feel the need to speak with the course instructor about an issue or team problem, what is our team's norm?}
We will first discuss openly within the team, to see if we can find a solution. If that doesn't work, we will have a member of the team be the news messenger and the one who will maintain contact with the instructor.

\section*{Psychological Safety}
\subsection*{What are three actions our team can take to foster psychological safety in the team?}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Give positive reinforcement to the team, especially when things get hard and complicated.
    \item Have an environment open to ideas and different perspectives. Nobody shall try to enforce their opinion or solution.
    \item Help each other and listen to other team member's struggles and preoccupations.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Decision Making}
\subsection*{Identify potential obstacles your team may face in terms of decision making.}
We have a team with a lot of members, all with different experiences and methods for working. This can make decisions complicated, especially if two persons or more have different opinions and they cannot come to an agreement. Since there is also a design aspect to this project, it might be an obstacle to decide on specific design choices, since not everyone has the same tastes.
\subsection*{How will your team ensure that your decision-making processes are accessible and equitable?}
When such arguments and decisions arise, we will be performing votes within the group chat in order to decide. This way, everyone has their say on the matter and a decision is taken in an equal way. 

\section*{Celebrate}
\subsection*{Describe ways the team will support and celebrate each other.}
Since we are all friends in the group, we will celebrate our achievements by organizing group activities, like going out to eat as a team. We will also support each other by highlighting our good actions and by allowing everyone to shine and contribute to the project.
